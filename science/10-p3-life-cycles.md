## Animal life cycles

| Type | Stages | Examples | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4-stage** | **egg → larva → pupa → adult** | butterfly, **mosquito**, **beetle**, housefly, mealworm | Larva **does not resemble** the adult; the larva **moults**; the pupa does not feed |
| **3-stage (with nymph)** | **egg → nymph → adult** | **cockroach**, **grasshopper**, dragonfly | Nymph **resembles** the adult but has **no wings**; nymph **moults** |
| **3-stage (egg → young → adult)** | egg → young → adult | **chicken** (chick), **frog** (tadpole), lizard | Chick **resembles** the adult; tadpole **does NOT** resemble the adult frog |
| Mammals | young (born alive) → adult | cat, human | **No egg stage** |

- Butterfly: larva = **caterpillar** (eats leaves, a **pest** to farmers), pupa = chrysalis.
- **Mosquito**: egg, larva and pupa live **in water**; the adult lives on **land**. The larva and pupa **breathe air at the water surface through breathing tubes**.
- Dragonfly nymph lives in water; adult on land.
- Frog: eggs laid **in water**; tadpole lives in water (gills); adult lives on land and in water (lungs).
- Moulting: shedding the outer covering so the young can **grow bigger**. Adults do **not** moult.

### Preventing mosquito breeding (dengue)
- **Oil on stagnant water**: the oil **blocks the breathing tubes** of the **larvae and pupae**, so they **cannot breathe** and **die** before becoming adults.
- **Remove stagnant water**: turn over pails, change water in vases, clear roof gutters. Then mosquitoes have **no place to lay eggs**.

### Method: bar graph of days per stage
Add the days **in order** (egg → larva → pupa → adult).
Example: egg 8, larva 12, pupa 6, adult 17 days.
- Days 1–8 egg; days 9–20 larva; days 21–26 pupa; day 27 onwards adult.
- **Day 17 → larva.**
- Time from hatching to adult = larva + pupa = 12 + 6 = 18 days.

## Plant life cycle

**Seed → germinating seed (root out first, then shoot) → seedling → young plant → adult plant (flowers → fruits → seeds)**

- **Germination** = a seed starting to grow.
- **Germination needs: water, air (oxygen), suitable temperature (warmth).** It does **not** need light or soil.
- The **root grows out first**, then the **shoot**.
- **Seed leaves** store **food** for the young plant. They **shrink** and fall off as the food is **used up**.
- Once its leaves are green, the seedling needs **light** to **make food** and continue growing.
- Seedlings grown in the **dark** become **tall, thin, weak and yellow**, and eventually die.

### Method: germination table
- Set-ups that germinated → list the conditions they **all** had.
- Set-ups that did not → find which condition was **missing**.
- Example: A (light, water) ✗; B (water, air) ✓; C (light, water, air) ✓ → **water and air are needed**; light is **not** needed (B had no light).

## Practice questions

**Q1 (MCQ).** Which has a 4-stage life cycle?
(1) beetle (2) chicken (3) grasshopper (4) frog
**A:** (1)

**Q2 (MCQ).** Which has a pupa stage?
(1) frog (2) chicken (3) mosquito (4) grasshopper
**A:** (3)

**Q3 (MCQ).** Cockroach nymph and adult: A both have feelers B nymph does not resemble the adult C nymph has no wings, adult has wings D nymph moults, adult does not.
**A:** **A, C and D.** (B is false: the nymph resembles the adult.)

**Q4 (MCQ).** Chicken (egg → young → adult) and frog (egg → young → adult). Similarities? A both 3 stages B both have an egg stage C the young of both resemble the adult D the young of both live on land and in water.
**A:** **A and B only.** A tadpole does not resemble the frog; a chick lives only on land.

**Q5.** Fill in the mosquito life cycle starting from adult. [1]
**A:** Adult → **egg → larva → pupa** → adult

**Q6.** Explain how spraying oil on stagnant water prevents mosquitoes from breeding. [2]
**A:** The oil **blocks the breathing tubes** of the **larvae (and pupae)**, so they **cannot breathe** and **die**, and do not grow into adults that can lay eggs.

**Q7.** 30 mosquito eggs at 22, 25, 28 °C: 18, 23, 28 hatched in a day. How does temperature affect hatching? [1]
**A:** **The higher the temperature of the surroundings, the more mosquito eggs hatched in a day.**

**Q8 (MCQ).** Bar graph: egg 8 days, larva 12, pupa 6, adult 17. Which stage on day 17 after the egg is laid?
**A:** **Larva** (days 9–20).

**Q9 (MCQ).** Bar graph: pupa 6 days, larva 9, egg 6, adult 10. Which conclusion is correct? (1) The pupa moults several times. (2) The insect is a pest for only 6 days. (3) Egg, larva and pupa live in water. (4) It takes 15 days to become an adult after the egg hatches.
**A:** (4). Larva 9 + pupa 6 = 15. (The others cannot be concluded from the graph.)

**Q10.** Insect W: egg (30 days) → nymph (120 days) → adult. Insect Z: egg (4) → larva (18) → pupa (10) → adult. Which are NOT correct? A Both lay eggs. B Z takes longer to become an adult. C More stages means a longer time to become an adult.
**A:** **B and C.** W takes 150 days, Z takes 32 days.

**Q11.** Animals: 3-stage eggs in water (A), 3-stage eggs on land (B), 4-stage eggs in water (C), 4-stage eggs on land (D).
(a) Similarity between A and C? (b) Difference between B and D? (c) Frog? Butterfly?
**A:** (a) **Both lay eggs in water.** (b) **B has a 3-stage life cycle but D has a 4-stage life cycle.** (c) Frog **A**; butterfly **D**.

**Q12 (MCQ).** Seeds germinated in set-ups with water + air and with light + water + air, but not with light + water only. Conclusion?
**A:** **Water and air are needed for germination.**

**Q13 (MCQ).** Bean seeds on wet cotton wool: plate X by the window, plate Y in a black box by the window. Aim?
**A:** To find out if the **presence of light** affects the **height of the seedlings**.

**Q14.** Bean plant growth with two missing stages: F → (seed with root) → G → young plant. F and G?
**A:** F = **seed**; G = **seedling** (with seed leaves and a short shoot).

**Q15.** Why do the seed leaves of a seedling shrink? [1]
**A:** The **food stored** in them is **used up** by the seedling to grow.

## Common mistakes
- Saying a grasshopper or cockroach has a pupa stage (it has a **nymph**).
- Saying the mosquito pupa lives on land (egg, larva and pupa are all **in water**).
- Saying seeds need **light** to germinate.
- Saying the **shoot** comes out first.
- Not adding days **cumulatively** in bar-graph questions.
