## 2.1 Systems

- An **organ** is a body part with a specific job (heart, lungs, stomach, brain).
- **Organs work together to form a system.** Systems work together to keep us alive.

| System | Main parts | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Digestive** | mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine, large intestine | **Breaks down food into simpler substances** that can be absorbed |
| **Respiratory** | nose, windpipe, lungs | **Takes air into and out of the body** (takes in oxygen, removes carbon dioxide) |
| **Circulatory** | **heart, blood, blood vessels** | **Transports digested food, water and oxygen** to all parts of the body |
| **Skeletal** | bones (skull, ribcage, backbone) | **Supports** the body and **gives it shape**; **protects** organs; works with muscles for **movement** |
| **Muscular** | muscles | Works with the skeletal system to **move** the body |

- Protection: **skull** protects the brain; **ribcage** protects the heart and lungs; **backbone** protects the spinal cord.
- Systems work together. Example: digested food absorbed in the small intestine is **carried by the blood (circulatory system)** to all parts of the body. Muscles **pull on bones** to move them.

## 2.2 Human digestive system

**Digestion** = breaking down food into **simpler substances** that can be **absorbed into the bloodstream**.

Path of food: **mouth → gullet → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → anus**

| Organ | What happens | Digestion? |
|---|---|---|
| **Mouth** | Teeth cut and chew food into smaller pieces. **Saliva** (digestive juice) moistens food and **starts digestion**. | ✅ **Begins here** |
| **Gullet** | Muscles push food to the stomach. | ❌ |
| **Stomach** | Churns and mixes food with **digestive juices**. | ✅ |
| **Small intestine** | Digestive juices **complete digestion**. **Digested food is absorbed into the bloodstream.** | ✅ **Completed here** |
| **Large intestine** | **Water is absorbed** from undigested food into the bloodstream. | ❌ |
| **Anus** | Undigested food (faeces) leaves the body. | ❌ |

- **Liver, gall bladder, pancreas** produce or store digestive juices for the small intestine. **Food does not pass through them.**
- Small intestine is **long and folded** (finger-like projections), giving a **large surface area** to absorb more digested food.

### Reading the "amount of digested food" bar graph (CHS)
- **Mouth = gullet** (same small amount): the gullet **does not digest food**, so no extra food is digested there.
- **Stomach**: more digested food. **Small intestine**: the most, since **digestion is completed** there.
- **Large intestine = zero**: digested food has **already been absorbed in the small intestine**, so none reaches the large intestine.

### Must-know table
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Digestion begins | Mouth |
| Digestion completed | Small intestine |
| Digested food absorbed | Small intestine |
| Water absorbed | Large intestine |
| Food passes, no digestion | Gullet, large intestine, anus |
| Produces juice, food does not pass | Liver, pancreas |

## Practice questions

**Q1 (MCQ).** A diagram shows all the bones of the body. Which system is this?
(1) skeletal (2) muscular (3) circulatory (4) respiratory
**A:** (1)

**Q2 (MCQ).** Which two statements describe the circulatory system?
A It absorbs digested food. B It breaks down food. C It is made up of the heart, blood and blood vessels. D It transports digested food and oxygen around the body.
(1) A and B (2) A and C (3) B and D (4) C and D
**A:** (4). A and B describe the **digestive** system.

**Q3.** Match each system to its function. [3]
Respiratory · Circulatory · Skeletal
→ supports the body and gives it shape / breaks down food / takes air into and out of the body / transports digested food, water and oxygen
**A:** Respiratory → takes air in and out. Circulatory → transports digested food, water and oxygen. Skeletal → supports the body and gives it shape. ("Breaks down food" is digestive, a distractor.)

**Q4 (MCQ).** In which part is digested food absorbed into the blood?
(1) gullet (2) stomach (3) large intestine (4) small intestine
**A:** (4)

**Q5.** Food from the stomach next passes to the ________. [1]
**A:** small intestine

**Q6.** A diagram labels G = mouth, H = gullet, J = stomach, K = large intestine, L = small intestine.
(a) In which parts does digestion take place? [1]
(b) State the function of K. [1]
**A:** (a) **G, J and L**. (b) It **absorbs water** from the undigested food into the bloodstream.

**Q7.** Why is the amount of digested food in the mouth and the gullet the same? [1]
**A:** The **gullet does not digest food**.

**Q8.** Why is there no digested food in the large intestine? [1]
**A:** All the digested food was **absorbed into the bloodstream in the small intestine**.

**Q9.** Why should we chew food well? [1]
**A:** Chewing breaks food into **smaller pieces**, so digestive juices can **digest it faster**.

**Q10.** Bread chewed for a long time tastes sweet. Explain. [1]
**A:** **Saliva** contains a digestive juice that breaks down starch in the bread into **sugar**.

**Q11.** Why is the small intestine long with many folds? [1]
**A:** It has a **larger surface area**, so **more digested food can be absorbed** into the bloodstream.

**Q12.** How do the skeletal and muscular systems work together? [1]
**A:** Muscles are attached to bones and **pull on the bones** to move them.

**Q13 (MCQ).** Which organ is protected by the ribcage?
(1) brain (2) stomach (3) lungs (4) small intestine
**A:** (3)

## Common mistakes
- Saying digestion happens in the **gullet** or **large intestine**.
- Saying food passes through the **liver/pancreas**.
- Mixing up: **small intestine absorbs digested food**; **large intestine absorbs water**.
- Saying the circulatory system "absorbs" food. It **transports** digested food.
- Saying the respiratory system "breathes food".
