# P4 Science – Final Exam Revision Guide

Built from:
- the contents pages of the P4 Science Notes & Practice book (`../1000017342.jpg`, `../1000017344.jpg`)
- 4 real school papers in this folder: **ACS (Junior) EOY 2023**, **Catholic High EOY 2023**, **Henry Park EOY 2023**, **Maris Stella WA1 2025**

## Exam format (from the EOY papers)

| | Booklet A | Booklet B | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type | 28 MCQ × 2 marks | 13 open-ended questions (1–3 marks per part) | 41 questions |
| Marks | 56 | 44 | **100** |
| Time | | | **1 h 45 min** (ACS, Henry Park) |

**Important:** the P4 end-of-year exam also tests **P3 topics** (living things, materials, life cycles, magnets). In the three EOY papers, about **40% of the questions** were P3 topics.

### Suggested time plan (105 min)

| Part | Time | Pace |
|---|---|---|
| Booklet A (28 MCQ) | 35 min | about 75 seconds per question |
| Booklet B (13 open-ended) | 55 min | about 4 minutes per question |
| Check everything | 15 min | unshaded OAS bubbles, units, keywords, "NOT"/"only" in questions |

## Structure of each topic file

1. **Seen in real papers**: the question types that actually appeared in the 4 papers.
2. **Key learning points**: what to memorise and understand.
3. **Method**: step-by-step approach for each question type.
4. **Practice questions with answers**: MCQ and open-ended, covering every learning point. Cover the answer and try first.
5. **Common mistakes**: traps that cost marks.
