Heat and temperature

Heat

A form of energy.

Temperature

A measure of how hot or cold something is. Unit: °C.

Numbers to remember

Water boils
100 °C
Human body
37 °C
Room (Singapore)
25–32 °C
Ice melts

Ice melts and water freezes at 0 °C.

Touch is not reliable. A hand from hot water feels warm water as cold. A hand from cold water feels the same warm water as hot.

2

Thermometers

Laboratoryabout −10 to 110 °C. For liquids and air in experiments.
Clinicalabout 35 to 42 °C. Body temperature only. It cannot measure boiling water.
DigitalShows the number quickly.
Sensor + dataloggerRecords temperature over time, accurately.

Using it correctly

  • Bulb fully in the liquid.
  • Bulb not touching the bottom or sides.
  • Hold it upright. Wait until the reading is steady.
  • Read at eye level while it is still in the liquid.
  • The liquid inside gains heat and expands, so it rises.
3

Heat flows from hot to cold

hotter— heat →colder
  • It keeps flowing until both are at the same temperature.
  • Gains heat → temperature rises. Loses heat → temperature falls.
  • A bigger difference in temperature → heat flows faster.

Mixing water

The answer is always between the two temperatures. Equal amounts of 80 °C and 30 °C → about 55 °C.

Biggest rise

A hot block raises the temperature most in the smallest amount of the coldest water.

4

Things end up at room temperature

  • A hot drink cools down to room temperature. It never goes below it.
  • A cold drink warms up to room temperature.
  • It cools fast at first (big difference), then slowly.
5

Amount of heat

Same temperature, more water

A pail of water at 60 °C has more heat than a cup at 60 °C.

Boiling

Less water boils first. It needs less heat to reach 100 °C.

6

Watch out!

✗The cold flows into my hand.
✓Heat flows out of my hand.
✗A hot drink cools to below room temperature.
✓It stops at room temperature.
✗80 °C water + 30 °C water = 110 °C.
✓The mix is between them: about 55 °C.
✗The thermometer bulb can touch the beaker.
✓Bulb in the liquid, not touching the sides or bottom.
✗More water has a higher temperature.
✓It has more heat at the same temperature.

Remember in 3 lines

Heat = energy. Temperature = how hot or cold (°C).
Heat flows hot → cold until they are the same temperature.
Things left out end at room temperature. Less water boils sooner.
