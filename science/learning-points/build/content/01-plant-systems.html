1

What is a system?

A system is made of parts that work together to do a job.

  • If one part stops working, the whole system may not work properly.
  • A plant is a system. Its leaves, stem, roots, flowers and fruits work together to keep it alive.
  • A bicycle is a system too: the pedals, chain and wheels work together to move it.
2

Plant parts and their jobs

LeafMakes food for the plant. Lets the plant take in and give out gases.
StemHolds the plant upright and holds the leaves up to the light. Carries water up and carries food to all parts.
RootsAbsorb water and mineral salts from the soil. Hold the plant firmly in the ground.
FlowerGrows into a fruit.
FruitProtects the seeds and helps spread them.

Which part is not working?

Leaves wilt

Not enough water reaches the leaves. The roots or the water-carrying tubes are damaged.

Plant falls over

The stem or roots are not giving support.

Plant slowly dies

Its leaves are gone, so no food is made.

3

Leaves: the food factory

A leaf's recipe for food

light+ water+ carbon dioxide→ food+ oxygen

The leaf takes in carbon dioxide from the air and gives out oxygen.

Why green?

Leaves have chlorophyll, which traps light. Only the green parts can make food.

Why flat and thin?

A larger surface area traps more light to make more food.

Parts of a leaf

Leaf blade, leaf stalk and veins. Veins carry water in and food out.

Special leaves

Cactus: leaves are spines that cut water loss, and the green stem makes food. Pitcher plant: leaves trap insects. Water lily: large leaves float on water.

Iodine test: is there food (starch)?

stays brown = no starch turns blue-black = starch
  • Green and white leaf: only the green part turns blue-black.
  • Part covered with black paper: the covered part stays brown, so light is needed to make food.
4

Stem: support and transport

Support

Holds the plant upright and the leaves up towards the light.

Transport

Water and mineral salts go up. Food goes to all parts.

Kinds of stems

Woody: hard and strong (mango tree)
Non-woody: soft and green (balsam)
Climbing: twines around a support or uses tendrils to reach more light
Creeping: grows along the ground (sweet potato plant)
Stores food: potato, sugar cane, ginger
Makes food: green stems (cactus)

Red-water experiment

Put a plant in red water. Red lines appear in the stem and leaves.

This shows that water travels up the stem in water-carrying tubes.

Ring experiment

The food-carrying tubes are in the outer layer, so food from the leaves cannot pass the ring.

The leaves do not wilt at first because the water-carrying tubes inside are still there.

5

Roots: drink and hold

Absorb

water and mineral salts from the soil

Anchor

hold the plant firmly in the ground

Store

some roots store food

Root hairs give a larger surface area, so the root absorbs more water.

Kinds of roots

Tap root: one main root (balsam, mango)
Fibrous roots: many thin roots (grass, rice)
Storage roots: carrot, radish, sweet potato
Prop roots: extra support (maize, mangrove)
Breathing roots: grow up out of the mud to take in air (mangrove)
Clinging roots: hold on to walls (money plant)
Aerial roots: hang in the air (orchid)

Tricky sort: stem or root?

Stem

  • potato
  • ginger
  • sugar cane

Root

  • carrot
  • sweet potato
  • radish
6

Experiment clues

These set-ups often come up in exams. Learn what each clue is for.

Layer of oil on the waterStops the water from evaporating. Then any drop in the water level is because the plant took in the water.
Roots in a plastic bagThe roots cannot touch the water, so the plant cannot absorb any.
Different number of leavesTests how the number of leaves affects how much water the plant takes in.
Plant kept in the darkTests whether light is needed to make food.

Seedling facts

  • The root grows out first, then the shoot.
  • Seed leaves shrink because the seedling uses up the food stored in them.
  • If all its leaves are cut off, the seedling dies. It cannot make food.
7

Watch out!

✗Roots take in food from the soil.
✓Roots take in water and mineral salts. Leaves make the food.
✗The shoot grows out of the seed first.
✓The root grows out first.
✗A potato is a root.
✓A potato is a stem. A carrot is a root.
✗The plant needs sunlight to grow.
✓The leaves need light to make food.
✗In the ring experiment, the water tubes were cut.
✓The food-carrying tubes (outer layer) were removed.
