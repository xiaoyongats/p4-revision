TransparentLets light through.
clear glass, clear plastic

Strong ≠ hard

Strong: does not break. Hard: does not scratch.

Flexible

Means it bends. It does not have to stretch.

3

Choosing the right material

WindowGlass: transparent, so we can see out. But it breaks easily.
UmbrellaFlexible + waterproof.
ShirtFlexible + absorbent (not waterproof, so it soaks up sweat).
TowelAbsorbent.
Nail, hammer headSteel: strong and hard, not flexible.
Bookshelf, chairStrong.
RaincoatWaterproof + flexible: plastic or rubber.
Life jacketFloats: styrofoam, cork.
Pot handlePlastic or wood: poor conductor of heat.
4

Testing materials

Bending test: masses added to a strip across two tables

  • Property tested: flexibility (bending without breaking).
  • The more mass added, the shorter the distance to the floor.
  • A bends more → A is more flexible → better for an umbrella canopy.

Strength test: weights on chairs (3 trials)

A: 4, 5, 3
B: 7, 9, 7
strongest
C: 2, 1, 1
  • More weights held before breaking = stronger. B is best for a bookshelf.
  • Changed variable: type of material.
  • Why 3 trials? To make the results more reliable.
5

Watch out!

✗Strong means hard to scratch.
✓Strong = does not break. Hard = does not scratch.
✗The strip that bends least is the most flexible.
✓The strip that bends most is the most flexible.
✗A shirt should be waterproof.
✓A shirt should be absorbent to soak up sweat.

Remember in 3 lines

Strong · hard · flexible · waterproof · absorbent · floats · transparent
Choose by the job: umbrella = flexible + waterproof; nail = strong.
In tests, quote the numbers. Repeat trials for reliable results.
