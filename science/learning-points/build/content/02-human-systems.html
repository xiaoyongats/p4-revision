1

Organs and systems

An organ is a body part with a special job. Organs work together to form a system.

DigestiveMouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine, large intestine.
Breaks down food into simpler substances that the body can absorb.
RespiratoryNose, windpipe, lungs.
Takes air into and out of the body: takes in oxygen and gives out carbon dioxide.
CirculatoryHeart, blood and blood vessels.
Transports digested food, water and oxygen to all parts of the body.
SkeletalBones: skull, ribcage, backbone.
Supports the body and gives it shape, protects organs, and helps us move.
MuscularMuscles.
Work with the bones to move the body.

The skeleton protects

Skull

protects the brain

Ribcage

protects the heart and lungs

Backbone

protects the spinal cord

2

Systems work together

Digestive + circulatory

Digested food is absorbed into the blood in the small intestine. The blood carries it to all parts of the body.

Skeletal + muscular

Muscles are attached to bones. Muscles pull on the bones to move them.

3

The path of food

Digestion means breaking down food into simpler substances that can be absorbed into the blood.

Mouth→ Gullet→ Stomach→ Small intestine→ Large intestine→ Anus

Orange border = digestion happens here.

Food does NOT pass through these

The liver, gall bladder and pancreas make or store digestive juices for the small intestine. Food never goes through them.

4

What each organ does

MouthTeeth cut and chew food into smaller pieces. Saliva is a digestive juice: digestion begins here.
GulletMuscles push food down to the stomach. No digestion.
StomachChurns and mixes food with digestive juices.
Small intestineDigestion is completed. Digested food is absorbed into the blood.
Large intestineWater is absorbed from the undigested food. No digestion.
AnusUndigested food leaves the body.

Why chew well?

Smaller pieces → digestive juices can digest the food faster.

Why is the small intestine long and folded?

A larger surface area → more digested food is absorbed.

Must-know answers

Digestion begins: mouth
Digestion completed: small intestine
Digested food absorbed: small intestine
Water absorbed: large intestine
Food passes, no digestion: gullet, large intestine, anus
Juices, but no food passes: liver, pancreas

6

Watch out!

✗Digestion happens in the gullet.
✓The gullet only pushes food. No digestion.
✗Food passes through the liver.
✓The liver and pancreas make juices. Food does not pass through them.
✗The large intestine absorbs digested food.
✓Small intestine absorbs digested food. Large intestine absorbs water.
✗The circulatory system absorbs food.
✓It transports digested food, water and oxygen.
✗Digestion begins in the stomach.
✓Digestion begins in the mouth (saliva).
