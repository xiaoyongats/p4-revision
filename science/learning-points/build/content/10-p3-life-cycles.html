1

4-stage life cycle

egg→ larva→ pupa→ adult

Examples: butterfly, mosquito, beetle, housefly, mealworm.

  • The larva does not look like the adult.
  • The larva moults (sheds its skin) to grow bigger.
  • The pupa does not eat.
  • Butterfly larva = caterpillar. It eats leaves, so it is a pest to farmers.
2

3-stage life cycles

With a nymph

egg→nymph→adult

cockroach, grasshopper, dragonfly

  • Nymph looks like the adult but has no wings.
  • Nymph moults. The adult does not.
  • Both have feelers.

Egg, young, adult

egg→young→adult

chicken (chick), frog (tadpole)

  • A chick looks like the adult.
  • A tadpole does not look like the adult frog.
  • Frogs lay eggs in water.

Mammals (cat, human) have no egg stage: the young are born alive.

3

Mosquitoes and dengue

In water

Egg, larva and pupa all live in water. They breathe air at the surface through breathing tubes.

On land

Only the adult lives on land and flies.

Oil on stagnant waterThe oil blocks the breathing tubes of the larvae and pupae. They cannot breathe and die before becoming adults.
Remove stagnant waterTurn over pails, change vase water, clear gutters. Mosquitoes have nowhere to lay eggs.

Warmer surroundings → more mosquito eggs hatch in a day.

4

Reading "days in each stage" graphs

Add the days in order. Example: egg 8 days, larva 12, pupa 6, adult 17.

  • Day 17 is in the larva stage (days 9–20).
  • Hatching → adult = larva + pupa = 12 + 6 = 18 days.
5

Plant life cycle and germination

seed→ germinating seed→ seedling→ young plant→ adult plant (flowers, fruits, seeds)

Germination needs

water, air, warmth.
It does NOT need light or soil.

Root first

The root grows out first, then the shoot.

Seed leaves

Store food for the young plant. They shrink as the food is used up.

After that

The seedling needs light to make food. In the dark it grows tall, thin, weak and yellow.

Germination table: what is needed?

A: light, waterdid not germinate (no air)
B: water, airgerminated (no light, still fine)
C: light, water, airgerminated

Conclusion: water and air are needed. Light is not.

6

Watch out!

✗A grasshopper has a pupa stage.
✓It has a nymph stage (3 stages).
✗The mosquito pupa lives on land.
✓Egg, larva and pupa are all in water.
✗Seeds need light to germinate.
✓Water, air and warmth. No light needed.
✗Reading a day graph bar by bar without adding.
✓Add the days in order to find the stage.

Remember in 3 lines

4 stages: egg → larva → pupa → adult (butterfly, mosquito, beetle)
3 stages: egg → nymph → adult (cockroach, grasshopper) or egg → young → adult (chicken, frog)
Germination needs water, air, warmth. Root first. Seed leaves shrink.
