## Key learning points

### Living vs non-living
Living things:
1. **need food, water and air**
2. **grow**
3. **respond** to changes around them
4. **reproduce**
5. eventually **die**

- **Bacteria are living things** (they grow and reproduce), even though they are too small to see.
- "Mass increases month by month" → it is **growing** → it is a **living thing**.

### Main groups
```
Living things
├── Plants ── Flowering plants (reproduce by SEEDS): papaya, hibiscus, banana, rice, balsam
│         └── Non-flowering plants: ferns & mosses (reproduce by SPORES); bird's nest fern
├── Animals ── mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, insects
├── Fungi ── mushroom, bread mould, yeast
└── Bacteria
```

### Animal groups

| Group | Body covering | Breathing | Reproduction | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mammals** | **hair / fur** | lungs | most **give birth to young alive**; feed young with **milk** | cat, lion, tiger, dog, zebra, whale, dolphin, bat |
| **Birds** | **feathers** | lungs | lay **hard-shelled** eggs | chicken, ostrich, penguin |
| **Reptiles** | **dry, scaly** skin | lungs | lay eggs (leathery shells) | turtle, tortoise, crocodile, snake, lizard, chameleon |
| **Amphibians** | **moist** skin | young: gills; adult: **lungs** (and skin) | lay eggs **in water** | frog, toad |
| **Fish** | **scales**, fins | **gills** | most lay eggs (guppy gives birth to young alive) | guppy, goldfish |
| **Insects** | hard outer covering | — | lay eggs | beetle, ant, butterfly, cockroach, mosquito |

**Insects:** **3 body parts** (head, thorax, abdomen), **6 legs**, **1 pair of feelers**.
⚠️ **Not all insects have wings**, so "has wings" is NOT a characteristic of all insects.
⚠️ A **spider** (8 legs, 2 body parts) is NOT an insect.
⚠️ Whale and dolphin are **mammals** (lungs, give birth to young alive). Bat is a mammal. Penguin and ostrich are **birds** even though they cannot fly.

### Plants
- **All plants make their own food** (they have green parts). Similarity between any two plants: "**can make their own food**".
- Flowering plants reproduce by **seeds** (in fruits). Ferns and mosses are **non-flowering** and reproduce by **spores**.

### Fungi
- **Cannot make their own food**: they feed on **dead or decaying matter** (or food such as bread).
- Reproduce by **spores**.
- Mould grows **faster** in **warm, moist/damp** conditions. Lower temperature (fridge) slows mould growth.

### Bacteria
- Very tiny; seen only with a **microscope**. Some are useful (making yoghurt); some cause diseases.

## Method: classification questions
1. **Heading for a group**: find what **all** members have in common (cat + zebra → **mammals**; mushroom + bread mould → **fungi**; banana tree + hibiscus → **flowering plants**).
2. **Flowchart missing question X**: X must be **true** for the "Yes" item and **false** for the "No" item, and must not be already used. e.g. chameleon (Yes) vs fish (No): "Does it have legs?" or "Does it live on land?" ✅. "Does it have scales?" ❌ because both have scales.
3. **"Based only on the information given"**: do not add your own knowledge.
4. **"Is it definitely an insect?"**: lays eggs + has legs is **not enough**. You need **3 body parts, 6 legs, 1 pair of feelers**.

## Practice questions

**Q1 (MCQ).** Living: dog, sunflower plant. Non-living: book, bacteria. Which is wrongly classified?
**A:** **Bacteria**. They are living things.

**Q2 (MCQ).** Which is NOT a characteristic of all insects?
(1) six legs (2) a pair of wings (3) a pair of feelers (4) three body parts
**A:** (2)

**Q3 (MCQ).** A papaya plant and a bird's nest fern are similar because they A reproduce by seeds B are non-flowering C can make their own food.
**A:** **C only.** The papaya is a flowering plant with seeds; the fern is non-flowering with spores.

**Q4 (MCQ).** A bar graph shows P's mass increasing from Jan to Apr. P is most likely a
(1) cat (2) robot (3) pencil (4) computer
**A:** (1). It grows, so it is living.

**Q5 (MCQ).** A lizard drops its tail when touched. This shows living things can
(1) grow (2) breathe (3) respond (4) reproduce
**A:** (3)

**Q6.** Headings: K = banana tree, hibiscus; L = mushroom, bread mould. [2]
**A:** K: **flowering plants**; L: **fungi**

**Q7 (MCQ).** Which is NOT a reptile?
(1) turtle (2) frog (3) crocodile (4) snake
**A:** (2). The frog is an amphibian.

**Q8 (MCQ).** Animal Y has lungs, moist skin and lives on land and in water. Group?
**A:** **Amphibians**

**Q9 (MCQ).** Flowchart: Has hair? Yes → squirrel. No → 3 body parts? Yes → beetle. No → X? Yes → chameleon; No → fish. Possible X?
(1) Does it lay eggs? (2) Does it live on land? (3) Does it have scales? (4) Does it live in water?
**A:** (2). Both lay eggs and both have scales; (4) would put the fish on the "Yes" side.

**Q10 (MCQ).** Which reproduces by seeds? fern / papaya / mushroom / bird's nest fern
**A:** **Papaya**

**Q11.** Group G: lion, earthworm, mushroom. Group H: marble, umbrella, pencil. Suggest headings. [2]
**A:** G: **living things**; H: **non-living things**

**Q12.** Mould was grown on bread at 5, 12, 18 and 25 °C. The bread at 25 °C had the most mould. State the relationship. [1]
**A:** **As the temperature increases, the growth of mould increases.**

**Q13.** Flowchart: "Does it give birth to young alive?" No → "Can it swim?" Yes → "Uses gills?" No → Q.
(a) Why can't Q be a mammal? (b) S gives birth to young alive; T does not. State a difference.
**A:** (a) Q **does not give birth to young alive**. (b) **S gives birth to young alive but T does not.**

**Q14.** Table: P has no legs, can swim, breathes in water, lays eggs. Q has legs, can swim, cannot breathe in water, lays eggs.
(a) Two similarities. (b) Maran says Q is definitely an insect. Agree? (c) Why can't Q live in water?
**A:** (a) Both **can swim** and both **lay eggs**. (b) **No.** We need to know that Q has **6 legs** (and 3 body parts). (c) It **cannot breathe in water**: it has no gills.

**Q15.** Wild cats are classified by plain hair (light/dark) or hair with patterns (spots/stripes). (a) Which group is a tiger in? (b) Which animal group are wild cats in? Give 2 reasons. (c) Can an ostrich be placed in this diagram?
**A:** (a) **Stripes**. (b) **Mammals**: they have **hair/fur** and **give birth to young alive** (and feed young with milk). (c) **No.** An ostrich is a **bird** (it has feathers and lays eggs); it is not a wild cat.

## Common mistakes
- Saying bacteria or fungi are non-living, or that fungi are plants. **Fungi cannot make their own food.**
- Saying all insects have wings.
- Classifying whales/dolphins as fish, or frogs as reptiles.
- In flowchart questions, choosing a question that is true for **both** animals.
- Adding outside knowledge when the question says "based only on the information given".
