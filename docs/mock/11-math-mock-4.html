A harder paper. Several questions have a trap: a missing digit to find, a NOT, a change of units, or a step that is easy to skip. The problem sums need careful bar models. No calculator.

  • Have paper and a pencil ready. Do your working on paper (draw bar models for problem sums), then type only the answer. No calculator.
  • Time plan: Section A about 20 min, Section B about 35 min, Section C about 35 min, then 15 min to check.
  • Typing answers: numbers like 2 315 or 2315, money like 5.40, fractions like 3/4 and mixed numbers like 2 3/4 (a space between). Fractions must be in simplest form.
  • After marking, each answer shows the full working. In the real exam, wrong answers with correct working can still get a method mark.
  • Your answers are saved as you go. When you finish, press Mark my paper at the bottom.
