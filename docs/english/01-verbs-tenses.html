Verbs and Tenses

The tense is told by time clue words in the sentence. Find the clue first, then choose the verb. Orange marks the clue words.

Find the time clue first

Simple presentevery day, always, usually, often, every morning → walks, has, does
Present continuousnow, at the moment, Look!, Listen! → am / is / are + -ing
Simple pastyesterday, last night, last week, ago, in 2020 → walked, went, bought
Past continuouswhile, when (something else happened) → was / were + -ing
Present perfectalready, just, yet, since, for, ever, never → has / have + done
Futuretomorrow, next week, later, soon → will + verb

No clue word? Look at the other verbs in the sentence or passage. A story told in the past keeps using the past.

2

Present tenses

Habits and facts
he / she / itadd -s: Grandpa jogs every morning.I / you / we / theyno -s: They jog every morning.
Happening right now
am / is / are + -ing"Please be quiet! I am reading a book now."
"We are eating now. Come and join us."
3

Simple past

Last night, my parents bought a present before going to the party.

Yesterday, Devi was surprised when her mother made pancakes.

was with I / he / she / it / one person · were with you / we / they / two or more people.

4

Was / were + -ing

A longer action that was going on when something else happened.

When Julia was walking to the toilet, she spotted a beehive.

While everyone was eating in the canteen, Jeremy went to the library.

5

Has / have + past participle

Something done before now that still matters now.

  • She has already finished her homework.
  • We have lived here for three years / since 2022.
  • He has just eaten.

has with he / she / it · have with I / you / we / they.

Do NOT use has/have with yesterday, last night, ago: "I have bought → bought it yesterday."

Watch out!

✗Last night, my parents have bought a present.
✓Last night → simple past: bought.
✗I read a book now.
✓now → am reading.
✗Last night, I were woken up by thunder.
✓I → was.
✗He has went home.
✓has + gone (not went).

Remember in 3 lines

Find the time clue: every day · now · yesterday · while · already · tomorrow.
Long action interrupted → was/were + -ing.
has/have + done, but never with yesterday or ago.
