3 items for $5. Buy 3, get 1 free. Bala has $145. How many items does he bring home?
sets$145 ÷ $5 = 29 setseach set3 bought + 1 free = 4 items
29 × 4 = 116 items

Method: list a few ways to buy, and compare. The cheapest price per item is usually best, but always check the leftover.

6

Watch out!

✗52 children, 8 per van → 6 vans.
✓4 children are left → need 7 vans.
✗Forgetting to subtract what was given away before dividing.
✓Do the steps in the order of the story.
✗Remainder bigger than the divisor (e.g. r 9 when ÷ 8).
✓The remainder must be smaller than 8.

Remember in 3 lines

× 2-digit: multiply by the ones, then the tens, then add.
Remainder: round up, ignore it, or it is the answer. Read the question.
Least cost: try the big deal first, then compare other ways.
