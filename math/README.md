# P4 Math – Final Exam Revision

## Exam format (2025 end-of-year papers)

| School | Section A (MCQ) | Section B (short answer) | Section C (problem sums) | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACS (Primary) | 15 × 2 = 30 | 20 × 2 = 40 | 8 questions, 30 marks | 1 h 45 min |
| Henry Park | 10 × 2 = 20 | 25 × 2 = 50 | 8 questions, 30 marks | 1 h 30 min |
| Catholic High | 20 × 2 = 40 | 20 × 2 = 40 | 5 questions, 20 marks | 1 h 45 min |

All are 100 marks. Problem sums are worth 3–4 marks each and **need working** (bar models, statements).

**Time plan for 1 h 45 min:** Section A 20 min · Section B 35 min · Section C 35 min · check 15 min.

## Suggested plan

1. One topic page per day (notes, then the quiz). Redo the quiz until 90%+.
2. Problem Sums Toolkit twice: bar models are where most marks are lost.
3. Then sit the 2025 papers timed (1 h 45 min), one every two days, and mark them with the answer keys.
4. For each wrong question, find the topic page and redo its quiz.
