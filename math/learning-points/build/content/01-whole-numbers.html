Range questions (very common)

A number is 2900 when rounded to the nearest ten. Numbers that round to 2900 go from 2895 to 2904.

Smallest = 2895 · Greatest = 2904. For the nearest hundred, 72 000 comes from 71 950 to 72 049.

Watch out!

✗"Value of 7 in 42 753" → 7
✓The value is 700. The digit is 7.
✗Fifty-eight thousand and thirty-nine → 5839
✓3 digits after the thousands: 58 039.
✗72 050 rounds to 72 000 (nearest hundred)
✓Tens digit is 5 → round up to 72 100.
✗Smallest number that rounds to 2900 is 2850.
✓Nearest ten → only ±5: 2895.

Remember in 3 lines

Digit ≠ value. 7 in the hundreds place has value 700.
Rounding: look one place to the right. 5 or more → go up.
Patterns: find the rule between terms, check it works for all of them.
