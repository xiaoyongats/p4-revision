1

What is a line of symmetry?

If you fold along the line, the two halves match exactly.

Each point on one side has a partner on the other side, the same distance from the line.

2

Letters and shapes

Symmetric lettersA, H, M, T, U, V, W, X, Y (up–down line) · B, C, D, E, K (side-to-side line) · H, X (both)
No line of symmetryF, G, J, L, N, P, Q, R, S, Z (N, S and Z only look the same when turned upside down)
Square4 lines (2 straight across, 2 diagonals)
Rectangle2 lines. The diagonals are NOT lines of symmetry.
Equilateral triangle3 lines
CircleEvery line through the centre
3

Completing a figure on a grid

Pick a square or corner→Count squares to the line→Count the same number on the other side

Blue squares are given; orange squares are their mirror partners, the same number of squares from the line.

4

Diagonal lines of symmetry

When the line goes corner to corner, the mirror square is found by moving across the line at right angles (slanting), not straight left or right.

For a line from the top-left to the bottom-right corner of a square grid: a square in row 2, column 5 has its partner in row 5, column 2. Swap the row and column numbers.

Squares that sit on the line are their own partner.

5

Watch out!

✗A rectangle has 4 lines of symmetry.
✓Only 2. Fold along a diagonal and the corners do not match.
✗S and N are symmetric.
✓They have no line of symmetry.
✗Shading "more squares" but forgetting the ones that were asked.
✓Shade exactly the number asked, then check every square has a partner.

Remember in 3 lines

Fold on the line → both halves match.
Square 4 lines · rectangle 2 · equilateral triangle 3.
Mirror partner: same distance from the line, on the other side.
