Word problems: factor or multiple?

"Greatest number of bags, none left over"Greatest common factor. 24 keychains and 32 bookmarks → 8 bags.
"Can be packed in 3s or 8s, none left over"Common multiple. Fewer than 60 → 24 or 48.
"Both buses leave together again"Smallest common multiple of the waiting times.

Watch out!

✗Mixing up factor and multiple.
✓Factor goes into the number; multiple is in its times table.
✗Forgetting 1 and the number itself as factors.
✓Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.
✗"Greatest number of bags" → using a multiple.
✓Sharing equally into groups → common factor.

Remember in 3 lines

Factors: find them in pairs. Multiples: the times table.
Common factor: in both factor lists. Common multiple: in both times tables.
Share into equal groups → factor. "Together again" / "packs of both" → multiple.
