Equivalent fractions and simplest form

Multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same number.

[[3/5]] = [[12/20]] (× 4) · [[50/75]] = [[2/3]] (÷ 25)

Simplest form: keep dividing until no number (except 1) divides both.

How many eighths in 1 whole? [[8/8]] = 1 → 8.

3

Comparing fractions

Same top

Bigger bottom = smaller fraction: [[5/6]] > [[5/9]].

Different everything

Make the bottoms the same: [[1/2]] = [[9/18]], [[5/6]] = [[15/18]], [[5/9]] = [[10/18]].

Decreasing order: [[5/6]], [[5/9]], [[1/2]]. Tip: compare with [[1/2]] first.

5

Fraction of a set or amount

÷ bottom→× top

[[7/9]] of 126 cows and sheep are cows → 126 ÷ 9 = 14, × 7 = 98 cows. Sheep = 126 − 98 = 28 (or [[2/9]] × 126).

Shaded shapes: 4 stars out of 9 shapes → [[4/9]]. Count all the shapes for the bottom.

Watch out!

✗[[7/12]] + [[1/3]] = [[8/15]] (adding tops and bottoms)
✓Same bottoms first: [[11/12]].
✗Leaving [[8/10]] or [[17/10]] when the question says "simplest form" or "mixed number".
✓[[4/5]], [[1 7/10]].
✗3 pizzas, ate [[1/2]] and gave [[1/4]] → [[1/4]] left.
✓3 − [[3/4]] = [[2 1/4]]. Start from 3 wholes.
✗Finding the fraction of the wrong group.
✓Ask: [[7/9]] of what? Underline the whole.
