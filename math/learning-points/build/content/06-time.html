1

The 24-hour clock

  • Always 4 digits, no am/pm: 07 05, 13 25, 21 30.
  • A day starts at 00 00 (midnight) and ends at 23 59.
  • 12 noon = 12 00. 12 midnight = 00 00.
2

Converting

pm → 24-hour

Add 12 to the hour: 3.45 pm → 15 45. 7.05 pm → 19 05.

24-hour → 12-hour

Hour above 12? Subtract 12 and write pm: 21 30 → 9.30 pm. 16 10 → 4.10 pm.

Morning

Same hour, add a 0 if needed: 8.15 am → 08 15.

Just after midnight

00 20 → 12.20 am.

3

Hours and minutes

1 h = 60 min (not 100!)

  • 2 h 15 min = 120 + 15 = 135 min
  • 85 min = 60 + 25 = 1 h 25 min
  • 1 h 40 min + 2 h 35 min = 3 h 75 min = 4 h 15 min
4

The timeline method

Draw a line. Jump to the next full hour first, then whole hours, then the minutes left.

Going backwards (start time): jump back to the full hour first. 15 35 − 50 min: 15 35 → 15 00 is 35 min, 15 more → 14 45.

5

Watch out!

✗14 45 + 1 h 25 min = 15 70
✓70 min = 1 h 10 min → 16 10.
✗Writing "13 25 pm"
✓24-hour time has no am/pm. 12-hour time needs am/pm.
✗Subtracting times like normal numbers: 18 10 − 16 40 = 170
✓Use a timeline: 1 h 30 min.
✗Answering in the wrong clock
✓Check: "using the 12-hour clock" or "24-hour clock".
