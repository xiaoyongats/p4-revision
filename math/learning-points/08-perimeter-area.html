The formulas

Perimeter = distance around

Rectangle: 2 × (length + breadth)

Square: 4 × side

Units: cm, m

Area = space inside

Rectangle: length × breadth

Square: side × side

Units: cm², m²

Square numbers to know (for "area → side")

Composite figures

Area: split or subtract

Split into rectangles and add, or take the big rectangle minus the cut-outs.

Perimeter: go round once

Steps that go in and out like stairs give the same perimeter as the big rectangle. A notch cut into a side adds its two inner sides.

A 36 cm × 17 cm paper has a 6 cm square and a 3 cm square cut from two corners. Area left?
big rectangle36 × 17 = 612 cm²cut-outs6 × 6 + 3 × 3 = 45 cm²left612 − 45
567 cm²
An 18 cm × 8 cm rectangle has a notch 4 cm deep cut into its bottom edge. Perimeter?
rectangle18 + 18 + 8 + 8 = 52notch sides4 + 4 = 8
60 cm

Watch out!

✗Perimeter 42 m, breadth 6 m → length = 42 − 6 = 36 m
✓Halve first: 21 − 6 = 15 m.
✗Area of a square of side 8 = 32 cm² (8 × 4)
✓8 × 8 = 64 cm². 8 × 4 is the perimeter.
✗Path 2 m wide: inner = 75 − 2
✓Both sides: 75 − 4 = 71.
✗Writing cm for area
✓Area is in cm² or m².

Remember in 3 lines

Perimeter: add all sides. Area: length × breadth (cm²).
Missing side from perimeter: halve first, then subtract.
Composite: split or subtract. Paths: outer area − inner area.
