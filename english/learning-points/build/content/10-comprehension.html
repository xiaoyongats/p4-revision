1

Steps

1. Read the passage once for the story→2. Read the question; underline the key words→3. Find the paragraph it points to→4. Answer using words from the passage→5. Check marks [2m] = 2 points
2

Question types

True / FalseFind the exact line. Opposite words make it False (made it to the finals ↔ did not qualify).
Tick the answerRead all choices; the right one matches the reason in the passage, not just a word.
Sequencing 1, 2, 3Number the events in the order they happened in the paragraph.
Who did it? (table)Find each action and the name next to it.
Word meaningReplace the word with each choice and see which keeps the meaning.
What does "it" refer to?Look at the sentence before; find the noun.
Why / How / What (open)Answer in a full sentence. "List two" = two separate points.
3

Full-sentence answers

Q: Why does Mr Ong call Madam Salmah?
✗ Late.
✓ Mr Ong calls Madam Salmah because the vegetables are going to be delivered late.
  • Use the question's words to start: "Why does Mr Ong call…" → "Mr Ong calls … because …".
  • Change I / my in the passage to he / she / his / her.
  • Keep the tense of the question (does → present, did → past).
  • Copy only the part that answers. Do not copy the whole paragraph.
4

Tick and sequence questions

Gita ran to the bus stop because she ___. wanted to talk to Madam Lim / did not want to see Sumei / wanted to avoid the rain ✓. The passage says it started raining and she had no umbrella.

Sequencing: look for time words: first, then, after that, finally, at 5 a.m.

5

Word-meaning questions

"Which word means 'worriedly'?" After thanking Madam Lim profusely (A), Gita walked home anxiously (B). → B.

Put the given meaning in place of each word. Only one keeps the sentence making sense.

6

Watch out!

✗Answering from what you think, not what the passage says.
✓Every answer must be proved by the passage.
✗One-word answers for "Why" questions.
✓Full sentence with "because".
✗Giving one point for a 2-mark question.
✓[2m] → two ideas.

Remember in 3 lines

Questions go in passage order. Find the paragraph, then the line.
Answer in a full sentence using the question's words; change I → he/she.
Count the marks: one point per mark.
