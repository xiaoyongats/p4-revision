1

Plan first (5 minutes)

  • Underline the topic (e.g. showing courage). Your story must clearly be about it.
  • Choose one main picture. It must be an important part of the story, not a small detail.
  • Answer the guiding questions (Why? How? How did you feel in the end?) in your plan.
  • Use 2–3 of the helping words.
2

The story shape (5 paragraphs)

1 IntroductionHook the reader: a sound, a speech, or a feeling. Who, where, when.
2 Build-upWhat led to the problem.
3 ProblemThe most exciting part. Longest paragraph. Feelings and actions.
4 ResolutionHow the problem was solved (by the main character, not by magic).
5 EndingFeelings and the lesson learnt, linked to the topic.
3

Show, don't tell

Tell
I was nervous.
Show
My palms were sweaty and my heart pounded like a drum as I walked onto the stage.
Tell
He was angry.
Show
His face turned red and he clenched his fists.
Tell
I was disappointed.
Show
My shoulders slumped and tears welled up in my eyes.

Use the 5 senses: what you saw, heard, smelt, felt and tasted.

4

Word toolbox

Openers"Watch out!" … · Crash! … · It was a day I would never forget.
Time linksAt first · Moments later · Suddenly · Without warning · After a while · In the end
Couragetook a deep breath · mustered my courage · did not give up · stepped forward
Disappointmentmy heart sank · lost for words · hung my head · felt miserable
LessonI learnt that … · From then on, I … · It taught me to …
5

Speech marks

"Can we have lunch now?" Julia asked.

"I am hungry," said Julia. → comma inside the speech marks, then said.

Julia said, "I am hungry." → comma before the speech, capital letter inside.

New speaker → new line.

7

Watch out!

✗A story about a fun trip when the topic is "a disappointment".
✓The topic must be the centre of the story.
✗"And then … and then … and then …"
✓Vary time links: Moments later, Suddenly, After a while.
✗"It was all a dream."
✓End with feelings and a lesson.

Remember in 3 lines

Plan: topic + one main picture + answers to the guiding questions.
Introduction → build-up → problem → resolution → ending with a lesson.
Show feelings with actions and senses. Past tense. Check at the end.
