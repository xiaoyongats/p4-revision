Feeling words (also great for compositions)

Very happyoverjoyed, thrilled, delighted, elated, ecstatic
Thankful / proudgrateful, appreciative, proud, relieved (worry is over)
Saddisappointed, miserable, heartbroken, upset, devastated
Scaredterrified, frightened, petrified, anxious, nervous, timid
Angryfurious, frustrated, annoyed, irritated
Otherconfused, embarrassed, curious, confident, courageous, remorseful (sorry for a wrong)
3

Ways to say, move and sound

Sayingwhispered (softly, a secret) · shouted / yelled (loudly) · muttered (unhappily, quietly) · stammered (nervous) · exclaimed (surprised) · pleaded (begging)
Waterdripping (drops, a leaking tap) · gushing (a lot, fast) · oozing (thick, slowly) · sloshing (moving about in a container)
Movingstrolled · dashed · tiptoed · trudged · wandered · scurried
Lookingglanced · stared · peeked · gazed · glared (angrily)
4

Word forms (endings)

Naming word (noun)Describing word (adjective)How (adverb)
honestyhonesthonestly
carecarefulcarefully
abilityableably
protectionprotectiveprotectively
dangerdangerousdangerously

After his / her / the → noun (his honesty). Before a noun → adjective (protective gear). After a verb, telling how → adverb (read carefully).

5

Look-alike words

remind / warn / adviseremind = help someone remember · warn = tell about danger · advise = suggest what to do
occasion / situationoccasion = a special event (a birthday) · situation = what is happening
ability / powerability to see clearly · power = strength or control
revise / renewrevised the recipe = changed it to improve it · renewed = made it valid again (a passport)
6

Watch out!

✗Choosing the word you like best.
✓Choose the word the clues support.
✗Reading only the words just before the blank.
✓The clue is often in the next sentence.

Remember in 3 lines

Underline the clues. Decide: happy or sad, good or bad.
Try every option in the blank and read it aloud in your head.
Check the word form: his ___ → noun · ___ gear → adjective · read ___ → adverb.
