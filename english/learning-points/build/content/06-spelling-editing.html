1

Adding -ed, -ing, -er

Short word, 1 vowel + 1 consonantDouble the last letter: stop → stopped · run → running · big → bigger
Ends in silent eDrop the e: make → making · hope → hoping · bake → baked
Consonant + yy → i: cry → cried · happy → happier · (but crying keeps y)
Vowel + yKeep the y: play → played · enjoy → enjoyed
-fulOnly one l: careful, beautiful, helpful. Then -ly: carefully
2

Plurals

-s / -es

book → books · box → boxes · bus → buses · watch → watches · dish → dishes

-y

baby → babies · city → cities · but toy → toys, key → keys

-f / -fe

leaf → leaves · knife → knives · shelf → shelves

Special

child → children · mouse → mice · tooth → teeth · sheep → sheep · fish → fish

3

ie or ei?

i before e, except after c

friend, believe, piece, field · receive, ceiling, deceive · Exceptions to learn: their, weird, neighbour, eight, height

4

to / too / twoto school · too hot / me too · two = 2
your / you'reyour bag · you're = you are
its / it'sits tail · it's = it is

weather / whethersunny weather · whether or not

lose / looselose a game · a loose tooth
5

Often misspelt

becausebeautifulbelievedifferentdisappearembarrassedenvironmentexcitedfavouritefinallyimmediatelyinterestinglibrarynecessaryneighbourprobablyrestaurantseparatesurprisedtomorrowuntilWednesday

Singapore uses British spelling: favourite, colour, neighbour, organise, realise, centre.

6

Editing your own work

Spelling→Tense (past in a story)→Agreement (was/were, is/are)→Capitals and full stops

Read slowly, one word at a time, pointing with your pencil. Mistakes hide when you read fast.

7

Watch out!

✗carefull · beautifull
✓careful, beautiful
✗stoped · runing
✓stopped, running
✗Their going to the park.
✓They're (they are) going to the park.

Remember in 3 lines

Double short words (stopped), drop silent e (making), y → i (cried).
-ful has one l. i before e except after c.
there/their/they're · to/too/two · its/it's.
