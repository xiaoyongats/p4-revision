2

Who or what does it refer to?

  • Boy → he / him / his / himself. Girl → she / her / herself.
  • Thing or animal → it / its. Many → they / them / their.
  • In speech marks, the speaker says I / me / my: Julia said, "Can we have lunch now? I am hungry."
  • "himself" points back to the same person: He took a photograph of himself.

its = belonging to it (The dog wagged its tail). it's = it is.

Watch out!

✗Me and him went home.
✓He and I went home.
✗too few chilli · too much people
✓too little chilli · too many people
✗Those seat here is reserved.
✓One seat, here → This seat.
