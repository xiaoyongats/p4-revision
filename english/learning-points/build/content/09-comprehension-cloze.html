What kind of word is missing?

before a noun (a / the …)adjective or article: a / an / the / some
after a person, before an objectverb (check the tense!)
between two ideasconnector: and, but, so, because, however, although
before a place or timepreposition: in, on, at, to, from, with
replacing a nounpronoun: it, they, them, their, who, which

Phrases to remember: blend in with · pay attention to · take care of · in spite of · as soon as · instead of · look forward to

4

Watch out!

✗Writing two words or a phrase.
✓One word per blank.
✗Present tense in a story told in the past.
✓Match the tense of the passage.
✗Forgetting a capital letter at the start of a sentence (However).
✓Capital letter if the blank starts the sentence.
