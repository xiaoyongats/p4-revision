Prepositions of time

ata clock time or point: at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night
ona day or date: on Monday, on 6 December, on my birthday
inlonger periods: in June, in 2025, in the morning
duringwhile something is happening: during the assembly, during recess
forhow long: for a week, for two hours (jogged every day for a week)
sincestarting point: since Monday, since 2022

in the box · on the table · at the bus stop · under · between · behind · beside · among the plants

Where it goes

into the room · out of · across the road · through the tunnel · along the path · towards

4

Connectors

and / both … andadds: creative and interesting
but / although / howeversurprise, opposite: lost her pen but did not cry
because / as / sincereason: stayed home because she was ill
soresult: she was ill, so she stayed home
or / either … orchoice
before / after / while / when / untiltime order

Test it: say the sentence with each option. Does the second part add, surprise, give a reason, or a result?

5

Watch out!

✗in Monday · on June · at the morning
✓on Monday · in June · in the morning
✗Although she was tired, but she kept going.
✓Use only one: Although she was tired, she kept going.
✗Because it rained. (alone)
✓We stayed indoors because it rained.

Remember in 3 lines

at a time · on a day · in a month/year · during an event · for how long.
Learn word partners: pay attention to, proud of, good at.
Connectors: and adds · but surprises · because gives a reason · so gives a result.
