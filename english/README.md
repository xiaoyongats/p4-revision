# P4 English – Final Exam Revision

## Exam format (2025 end-of-year papers: ACS, Henry Park)

| Paper | What | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | Composition: at least 120 words on a topic, using one or more of 3 pictures (50 min) | 20 |
| Paper 2 (1 h 15 min) | Vocabulary MCQ 6 · Grammar MCQ 10 · Visual text MCQ 4 · Grammar cloze 8 (word bank + brackets) · Comprehension cloze 4 · Synthesis 4 · Comprehension 1 (8) · Comprehension 2 (10) | 50 |
| Listening | Listening comprehension | 14 |
| Oral | Reading aloud + conversation | 16 |
| **Total** | | **100** |

Spelling/editing is not a separate section in the 2025 papers, but spelling still costs marks in composition and comprehension answers.

## Topic pages (in the app under the English tab)

| Group | Pages | Exam section |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar | 1 Verbs & Tenses · 2 Subject–Verb Agreement · 3 Pronouns & Quantifiers · 4 Prepositions & Connectors | Grammar MCQ, Grammar cloze |
| Words | 5 Vocabulary · 6 Spelling & Editing | Vocabulary MCQ; writing accuracy |
| Sentences | 7 Synthesis & Transformation | Synthesis |
| Reading | 8 Visual Text · 9 Comprehension Cloze · 10 Comprehension (open-ended) | Visual text, Comprehension cloze, Comprehension |
| Writing & speaking | 11 Composition · 12 Oral & Listening | Paper 1, Oral, Listening |

Quiz types: choose (MCQ), fill in a word, and write a sentence. Sentences are checked against model answers (capital letter required). If a child writes a different but correct sentence, they can tap "My answer means the same" to give themselves the mark, so it is worth a parent glancing at those. Comprehension answers are marked by the key ideas.

## Papers in `papers/` (kept local, not uploaded)

- 2025 end-of-year (SA2): ACS, Henry Park, Catholic High, Nanyang, Ai Tong, Nan Hua (testpapersfree.com)
- 2025 ACS WA2 and 2023 ACS EOY, Raffles Girls PP, Catholic High WA2/WA3 (tutorly.sg)

Answer-key notes: ACS 2025 Q30 gives "The flowers, which are pretty are in the vase."; the better answer is **The flowers which are in the vase are pretty.** Henry Park 2025 keys contain small typos ("tried" for "tired").
